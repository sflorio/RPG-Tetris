# Design alignment: prototype vs. the projectfour vault

Read against the design vault at `publish.obsidian.md/projectfour` (27 notes),
this records where the current prototype matches the design, where it actively
conflicts, and the order I'd change things in.

**Summary:** the overworld foundation is fine. The combat prototype is built on
assumptions the design contradicts — one board instead of two, damage from line
counts instead of from characters, and a combo system that doesn't exist in the
design. Most combat code written so far is a throwaway spike, and that's OK: it
proved the RPG↔puzzle seam works.

---

## 1. What the design actually specifies

### Combat shape
- **Two boards**, each **10 wide × 40 tall**, lower 20 visible, upper 20 hidden
  and used to spawn active and junk blocks.
- Player + active team on the **left** board; enemy team on the **right** board.
- **Junk blocks are seeded on both boards at combat start** so neither starts empty.
- **Active Team = up to 3 units** per side.
- A **Round** ends each time the player completes a block drop. Status effects,
  damage-over-time and timers tick per round.

### Damage comes from characters, not from line counts
Each active character has **assigned Block Types**. Clearing a line with a block
matching a character triggers *that character*:

| Lines cleared | Result |
|---|---|
| 1 | That character's **Basic Attack** |
| 2 | That character's **Special Attack** |
| 3 | **Special Attack +50%** damage |
| 4 | **All** active characters perform their **Rally Strike** |

- 4-line clears are **only possible with the Line (I) block**.
- The I block is a **special block for triggering Rally Strikes**, not a normal
  damage piece, and Anima are not assigned to it.
- **Union meter**: fills as lines are cleared. At **10 total lines** the team's
  **Union Assault** fires automatically and the meter resets. Up to 13 lines can
  land in one threshold; each line past 10 adds **+10% damage**.

### Stats
Per unit: `NAME, LVL, TYPE, TYPE2, HP, POW, DEF, BAR, PSC, PSP, DOD`.
Party-wide: `LVL, EXP, TP, LUCK, CRWN, TIME, BATL, GRAV, MOVE, ENCR`.

**`GRAV` (Gravity) is a party stat** that sets block fall speed and is modified by
Stars and enemy attacks. Fall speed is not derived from enemy stats.

### Status effects that drive the board
| Effect | Board consequence |
|---|---|
| Blind | 33% of the board hidden by fog |
| Shocked | Team **cannot rotate** their active block |
| Confusion | Block preview obscured |
| Infection | 30% chance per infected unit to generate a **Xenoblock** |
| Haste | That unit's block is guaranteed next |
| Bleed | Damage **each time a block is rotated** |
| Burning | Blocks decay over rounds, leaving holes |
| Frozen | Blocks must be cleared twice; first clear deals no attack |
| Thorned | Clearing damages the clearing character |
| Golden / Charged | Clearing heals HP / restores TP |

### Xenoblocks
Malformed blocks with `+`, `#` and `-` variants per shape (`O+`, `T#`, `J-` …).
Generated by Infection or enemy abilities.

### Controls
Two keyboard schemes plus controller. Combat: `A/D` move + `←/→` rotate in
scheme A, and the reverse in scheme B. `Space` = Hold/Swap. `W/↑` hard drop,
`S/↓` soft drop. `F` techniques, `R` inventory, `M` map, `Esc` cancel.

### Progression and meta
Techniques (TP-gated, 2 equipped per character, level to 10), Rally Strikes,
Union Assault, Soul Gems (stat mods), Trinkets (triggered effects),
Constellations + Stars (difficulty/reward modifiers, grant Gravity), Xenoshards
(loot currency), Psionic Powers, Field Actions (one per recruited character),
Campfires (save + spend Mastery Points), Basecamp characters.

### Psionic Powers change the combat UI
These are **unlocks**, not defaults:
- `HP Sight` — enemy HP bars become visible
- `TP Sight` — enemy TP bars become visible
- `Future Sight` — enemy cast bars become visible
- `Third Eye` — next blocks shown for **both** boards
- `True Sight` — Perfect Strike prediction on targeting

## 2. Where the prototype conflicts

| # | Prototype today | Design | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **One** board, 10×23 (3 hidden) | **Two** boards, 10×40 (20 hidden) | Fundamental |
| 2 | Enemies are passive HP bars | Enemy team plays its own board | Fundamental |
| 3 | Damage = lines × multipliers | Damage = character attacks keyed to block type | Fundamental |
| 4 | Combos, same-piece streak ×3, back-to-back, perfect clear | None of these exist in the design; Union meter + Rally Strikes instead | High |
| 5 | I block is an ordinary piece | I block is the **Rally Strike trigger**; only it can clear 4 | High |
| 6 | Enemy HP bars always visible | Gated behind the `HP Sight` psionic power | Medium |
| 7 | Fall speed derived from enemy `base_speed` | Party `GRAV` stat, modified by Stars | Medium |
| 8 | Rotate `X`/`Z`, Hold `Shift` | Rotate `←/→` (or `A/D`), Hold `Space` | Low (fixed — see §4) |
| 9 | No Round concept | Round = one completed drop; effects tick per round | High |
| 10 | Enemy = OpenRPG `Battler` (bugcat, wolf) | Units with `TYPE/TYPE2`, `POW/DEF/BAR/PSC/PSP/DOD` | Medium |
| 11 | No status effects | 14 effects, most of which alter the board | High |
| 12 | 7 fixed block colours | 7 blocks × 3 Xenoblock variants | Medium |

## 3. What survives

Worth keeping rather than rewriting:

- **The combat seam.** `FieldEvents.combat_triggered(arena)` →
  `CombatEvents.combat_finished(won)` is untouched by any of this. Two boards and
  character attacks slot in behind the same contract.
- **Junk blocks at combat start.** Already implemented as `garbage_rows`; the
  design wants exactly this on both boards. Rename to *junk*.
- **SRS rotation and wall kicks.** `Raw Ideas` explicitly asks to follow SRS;
  PokeTetris already implements it with kick tables.
- **The whole overworld**: grid movement, maps, dialogue, encounters, save.
- **`BlockTextures`** as the single place block appearance is defined — it will
  need Xenoblock and status-effect variants (gold, charged, burning, frozen).
- **The damage-rule structure** (`Contribution`, labelled bonuses) is reusable as
  the *attack resolution* pipeline, even though every current rule is wrong.

## 4. Change plan

### Phase 0 — cheap and unambiguous — **done**
- [x] Remap combat controls to the spec, with selectable Keyboard A / B schemes.
- [x] Delete the pokeball sprites and the `PokeballTextures` autoload.
- [x] Rename `garbage_rows` → `junk_rows`.
- [x] Replace the red pixel frame, the Kremlin and the DOS font with the
      flat-modern board style, applied per board instance by `BoardSkin`.

### Phase 1 — board geometry — **done**
- [x] Widen the board to **10×40**, 20 visible, 20 hidden spawn area.
- [x] Introduce the **Round** concept: `round_finished(n)` after every drop.

### Phase 2 — units and attacks — **done**
- [x] `UnitStats`: `NAME/LVL/TYPE/TYPE2/HP/POW/DEF/BAR/PSC/PSP/DOD`.
- [x] `PartyStats`: `LVL/EXP/TP/LUCK/CRWN/GRAV/MOVE/ENCR` + Psionic Powers.
- [x] Assign **Block Types to characters**; clearing routes to that character.
- [x] Basic / Special / Special+50% / Rally Strike by line count.
- [x] **Union meter** (10 lines → Union Assault, +10% per line over).
- [x] Delete combo / streak / back-to-back / perfect-clear rules.
- [x] Gate enemy HP bars behind `HP Sight`.
- [ ] Tune the damage formula — `attack_resolver.gd` is a first pass, since the
      vault specifies which attack fires but not the maths.

### Phase 3 — second board — **done**
- [x] Two boards on screen: player left, enemy right, both seeded with junk.
- [x] Enemy board is **scripted, not simulated** ([EnemyBoard]). Its stack shows how
      close the next enemy attack is: it rises as they charge and drops when they strike.
- [x] Enemies charge over Rounds and attack when their cast completes.
- [x] Allies take damage, can be downed, and the battle is lost when the whole
      active team is down (as well as on a top-out).
- [x] Ally roster with health; enemy cast bars gated behind Future Sight, and the
      enemy's next blocks behind Third Eye.
- [ ] Enemy variety: every enemy currently uses a Basic Attack on a speed-derived
      timer. Real attack tables, Techniques and status application come with Phase 4.

### Phase 4 — status effects and Xenoblocks — **framework done, 9 of 14 effects live**
- [x] Effect framework with `-` / normal / `+` tiers, ticking once per Round.
- [x] Compound effects: Renew cancels with Bleed and with Poison, clearing both.
- [x] Shield (halves damage, consumed by stack), Renew, Poison, Bleed (on every
      rotation), Haste (spent per block generation, not per Round).
- [x] Board effects carried by units: Shocked (no rotation), Confusion (preview
      hidden), Blind (a third of the playfield fogged, chosen by team position).
- [x] Enemies inflict effects on hit; Rally Strikes grant Shield.
- [x] Effects shown in both rosters, tinted by whether they help or hurt.
- [ ] **Per-block effects** — Golden, Charged, Burning, Frozen, Thorned. All five
      need the board to store a *state per cell* alongside its colour, which is a
      change to `Grid` itself. That is the next meaningful chunk.
- [x] **Xenoblock space and rotation.** Settled against SRS: wall kicks are a
      property of the bounding box and rotation centre, never of which cells are
      filled, so a Xenoblock needs no rotation code of its own. `scr/XenoBlocks.gd`
      holds the shapes; the board's existing `getPosibleRotation` already picks
      the kick table by box size. Verified with a real wall kick on a J+.
- [x] **The `-` forms are decoded.** The vault reuses artwork across them
      (O-=L-, T-=Z-, J-=S-), which means they are trominoes — every `-` except
      `I-` is the same L-tromino at a different spawn orientation.
- [ ] **`+` shapes are placeholders** and **`#` shapes are unspecified.** These are
      the last thing blocked on the design rather than on code — see §5.
- [ ] **Infection**, which needs the above.

### Phase 5 — meta systems
Techniques/TP, Rally Strikes, Soul Gems, Trinkets, Constellations + Stars,
Xenoshards, Psionic Powers, Field Actions, Campfires, Basecamp, Menus.

## 5. Open questions

1. ~~**Does the enemy board actually play Tetris?**~~ **Resolved: scripted.** The
   enemy board is a readable picture of enemy pressure rather than a simulation.
   The seam is one method (`EnemyBoard.set_charge`), so a real AI could drive the
   same board later if it ever earns its cost.
2. **Block Types per character.** With 3 active characters and 7 block types,
   how is the mapping decided — fixed per class, assigned by the player, or
   rotating? And what happens when a clear uses a block assigned to nobody?
3. **Keyboard A vs B** — a player-selectable option in the Options menu, or a
   one-time choice? They can't coexist, since `A`/`D` mean *move* in one and
   *rotate* in the other.
4. **Square strength.** `Raw Ideas` floats "all pieces except Square can clear 3+
   lines" and "Square pieces are naturally a bit stronger". Is that settled?
5. **Xenoblock cell layouts.** Rotation is solved; the shapes are not. The `-`
   forms were recoverable from the vault's reused artwork (they are trominoes),
   and the `+` forms are in as reasonable pentominoes, but both want confirming
   against the design's images. **The `#` forms cannot be guessed at all** — they
   exist only for T/J/L/S/Z, no two share artwork, and nothing in the text says
   what `#` corrupts. Those need writing down as filled cells in a 3x3 or 4x4 box.
6. **Whose board does a unit effect fog?** Blind is a Unit Effect whose effect is
   on the board. Currently any afflicted ally fogs a third of the team's board,
   picked by that ally's position in the team. Worth confirming that reading.

## 6. Inconsistencies found in the vault

Worth fixing in the docs themselves:

- **Combat → Rotate Left and Rotate Right are both `A` on controller.** Presumably
  one should be `B` or a shoulder button.
- **Controller `X` is bound twice in combat**: Hold/Swap and Open/Close Map.
- **`R` is bound twice in combat**: Open/Close Inventory and Quick Field Action.
- **Blind** is listed under Unit Effects but its stated effect is on the board.
  Intentional ("Some Unit Effects will affect the Board"), but worth being
  explicit about how unit position determines which third is fogged.
- **Frozen / Thorned / Charged** have no `-` / `+` tiers defined, unlike their
  siblings.
- `Type Chart`, `Union Assault`, `Game Introduction`, `Examine Icon` are stubs;
  `Story` is marked TBD.
