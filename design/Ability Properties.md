**Name**
- The name of the ability.
**Type**
- The type of damage that the ability deals. This determines the effectiveness of a given ability based on the target's Type.
**Class**
- The class of damage that the ability deals. This determines if the ability uses the attacker's Offense or Technical Offense stat, along with the target's Defense or Technical Defense stat.
**Level**
- The level of the ability. Basic Attacks, Advanced Attacks, Techniques and Rally Strikes can all be leveled up by spending Mastery Points.
**Power**
- The raw damage of the ability. 
**Targets**
- The number of targets of the ability.
- One Ally, All Allies, One Enemy, All Enemies, All Units, Random Unit
**Effects**
- The targets and status effects/board changes of the ability.
**Description**
- A flavor text description of the ability.
