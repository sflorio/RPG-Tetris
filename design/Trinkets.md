Trinkets are also equippable items for allies, however unlike Soul Gems, these provide unique effects that can trigger during combat.

## Trinkets List

| Trinket Name   | Effect                                                                                               |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Warden's Chain | While active, if an ally with less HP would take damage, that damage is redirected to equipped ally. |
| 5-Leaf Clover  | Increases equipped unit's Luck by 1.                                                                 |
