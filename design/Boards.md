Combat in the game takes place on a two puzzle boards that are 10 blocks wide and 40 blocks tall, with the lower 20 vertical blocks being visible at all times and the upper 20 being hidden and used spawn new active blocks and junk blocks.

The player and their active team control the left side board, and the enemy team controls the right side board. When combat begins, a random amount of junk blocks are spawn on both boards (this is to prevent combat starting "empty").
## Clearing Lines
Characters in the active team will perform Basic Attacks, Special Attacks, and Rally Strikes as a result of clearing lines. If a Block Type is used to clear a line that matches one of the active team characters assigned Block Types, that character will perform accordingly:
- Clearing a single line will cause the character to perform their Basic Attack
- Clearing two lines will cause the character to perform their Advanced Attack
- Clearing three lines will cause the character to perform their Advanced Attack with a +50% bonus to damage
- Clearing four lines (only possible with Line Block) will cause all active team characters to perform their [[Rally Strikes]]
- Clearing ten lines will automatically trigger the Party's [[Union Assault]]

## Combat Start
When a combat encounter is initiated, both the player's board and the enemy board are generated with a random amount of "junk" blocks. These blocks are filler for the board but otherwise act as normal blocks to be cleared.

Start of combat junk block arrangements should never exceed the fifth line.
