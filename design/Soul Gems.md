Soul Gems are equippable items for allies, providing modifiers to their stats. Each character can equip one Soul Gem, but a Soul Gem can have up to four modifiers at once. Not all modifiers are positive, and in some cases a Soul Gem may be strong in some areas and detrimental in others.

Soul Gems are formed as a combination of their Soul Gem Type and Soul Gem Form.

Soul Gems can be acquired in multiple ways, including purchasing from a shop or receiving as loot from combat.
