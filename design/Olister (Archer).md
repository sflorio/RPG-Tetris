### Description
The player meets Olister shortly after escaping the Emberlight Inn, rescuing them from a group of aliens. Olister is relieved to be saved and happy to join the party, not knowing of the player's secret psion nature. 
### Trait
**Vicious Aim**: For each line cleared with Olister, he gains a stack of Vicious Aim. Vicious Aim increases his Perfect Strike Chance by 1% per stack and lasts until the end of combat.

### Recruitment
None, the player gains Olister to the party as part of the initial escape quest.
### Personal Quest
Olister dreams of making their name known across the kingdom as the best marksman.

**Requirement**: Olister must be in the player's party when checking the Quest Board in town. He will notice a flyer for an Archery Duel and must fight three other archer enemies in one on one combat.

**Reward**: Olister now has a 50% chance to gain two stacks of Vicious Aim when clearing a line.
