Xenoshards are gotten as loot from defeated enemies, and can be used in a multitude of ways:
- Sold to The Archivist. The first time a unique Xenoshard is provided, a Memory Scroll will be received. For subsequent duplicates, the player will receive crowns.
- Given to The Collector. The Collector only wants one of each type of Xenoshard, and will reward the player will a variety of prizes as collection thresholds are met.
- Put into the Transmuter. In exchange, the Transmuter will give the player a random Star, Soul Gem, or other loot. The quality of the loot given is dependent on the Xenoshard provided.

Quality Progression:
Cracked > Normal > Pristine
