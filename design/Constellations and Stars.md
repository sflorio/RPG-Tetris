Constellations and Stars provide the player with a way to modify the difficulty of the game while reaping appropriate rewards. Constellations themselves are only a series of nodes for Stars, however the layout of the Constellation may impact how efficient a Star can be. For example, a Star may have "All connected Stars gain Power+", with one Constellation having more connecting Stars than another.

Stars have a variety of effects associated to them. While Constellations are an item with fixed locations and methods of acquiring, Stars can be found as treasure, looted from defeated enemies, and purchased from vendors.

Constellations (and Stars) are equipped to the Party and affect multiple parts of gameplay.

Star Quality Progression:
Dim > Shining > Radiant > Brilliant

Star Heat Progression:
Red > Orange > Yellow > White > Blue
