The player's Inventory is divided into different sections depending on the type of item:
- All consumable items (that is, items that can be used and consumed) are placed in the Backpack
- All permanent or semi-permanent items (letters, quest items, keys) are placed in the Keepsakes
- All equippable items (Soul Gems, Trinkets, Constellations, Stars) are also placed in the Backpack
	- Constellations and Stars can be used at a Campfire to learn them
	- Soul Gems and Trinkets must be equipped, sold from the Backpack, or placed in Storage
## Backpack
The player's Backpack is initially limited to only 10 slots, but can be increased throughout the game by upgrading the Backpack. Each Backpack upgrade increases the number of slots by 3, shown as a + next to the name (Backpack++ would have 16 slots). At maximum (Backpack ![[Pasted image 20260930174733.png|20]]) the player will have 25 slots for consumables.

If the team would acquire an item while the Backpack is full, the item is sent to the Storage Box automatically. If the Storage Box is full when this happens, the player is notified and the item is discarded.
## Keepsakes
There is no limit to the number of Keepsakes the player can have in their Inventory. Keepsakes are things like notes found during the name, temporary important items like quest items or dungeon keys, Alien Glyphs, Xenoshards and more.
## Storage Box
The player has access to a Storage Box each Inn, allowing them to store or remove items as needed. This Storage Box is persistent across all Inns and is limited to 99 slots.
