While in combat, the player has access to up to six Characters assigned to the six Block Types available to the player. Of these six Characters, the player can select three as their Active Team. The remaining three compose the player's Support Team. Together, these form the player's Party.

#### Active Team
Characters in the Active Team will perform Basic Attacks, Advanced Attacks, and Rally Strikes as a result of clearing lines. If a Block Type is used to clear a line that matches one of the active team characters' assigned Block Types, that character will perform accordingly:
- Clearing a single line will cause the character to perform their Basic Attack
- Clearing two lines will cause the character to perform their Advanced Attack
- Clearing three lines will cause the character to perform their Advanced Attack with a +50% bonus to damage
- Clearing four lines (only possible with Line Block) will cause all active team characters to perform their Rally Strike

Only characters that participated in the Active Team will receive the unit based rewards after combat.
#### Support Team
Characters in the Support Team do not perform any functions by default, and are primarily a reserve of characters to use as additional combat options or resources.

Support Team characters do not gain unit based rewards after combat if they are not present in the Active Team for at least one round.
#### Tag Out
Characters in the Support Team can be called upon to take the place of an Active Team character through Tagging. To tag a Support Team character in, the player selects the Tag Out option at the top of the Techniques menu, then selects the Active Team character to Tag Out, and then selects the Support Team character to Tag In. This immediately swaps the two characters and puts Stun- on the character tagged in.

When a character in the Active Team is Downed, they can still be used as a target for Tag Out.
