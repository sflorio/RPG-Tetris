Alien Orbs contain mission/goal information from the alien hive. Picking an Alien Orb up will cause the player to learn a new Alien Glyph and to witness a cutscene involving the aliens that applies the new glyph and all known glyphs.

At the end of the game, the player will be able to watch all cutscenes with full context.
