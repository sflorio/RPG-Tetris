Techniques are abilities provided by the current Party of Combat Characters. The player can choose to use these at any time as long as they have the necessary Tech Points to perform it. Each Character can learn multiple Techniques, but can only equip (and provide) two at a time. If a Character is downed, their provided Techniques are locked until they are revived.

## Learning Techniques

Info on learning Techniques.

The most common way to learn Techniques is by spending Mastery Points at a Campfire, though they can also be learned through Memory Tomes.

Techniques can be leveled up by spending Mastery Points at a Campfire. Techniques can be leveled up to a maximum of 10, at which point the Technique is considered "Mastered" and gains a new effect.
