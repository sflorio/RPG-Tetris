| Term         | Definition                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Active Team  | The (up to) three units on either the ally or enemy team that are currently in combat.                                                                                                       |
| Block        | One of various shapes that are dropped onto or generated onto the game board.<br><br>Standard blocks are O, T, J, L S, and Z. I (line) acts as a special block for triggering Rally Strikes. |
| Party        | The Active Team and Support Team and collectively referred as the player's Party.                                                                                                            |
| Round        | Each Block drop the player completes results in Round ending.                                                                                                                                |
| Support Team | The (up to) three characters on the player's team that are not currently in combat.                                                                                                          |
| Unit         | Allies and enemies are collectively referred to as Units.                                                                                                                                    |
| Xenoblock    | Corrupted Blocks with malformed shapes, tagged as O+, T+, J+, L+, S+, Z+, and I+. There are also Xenoblock- and Xenoblock# forms of some Blocks.                                             |
