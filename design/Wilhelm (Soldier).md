### Description
The player meets Wilhelm during the initial invasion, saving him (and him saving them) in the process. Though he knows he should arrest the player for being a psion, he acknowledges that they are the only way they can survive the war.
### Trait
**Shieldbearer**: When dealt direct damage, Wilhelm has a 5% to ignore the damage and any effects.

### Recruitment
None, the player begins the first combat with Wilhelm.
### Personal Quest
Wilhelm desires nothing more than to honor his king and his family.

**Requirement**: Wilhelm must be in the player's party during a conversation with King Erigar. After this, Wilhelm must be in the player's party when they are met in Winehaven.

**Reward**: Wilhelm's Shieldbearer Trait now has a 15% chance to trigger.
