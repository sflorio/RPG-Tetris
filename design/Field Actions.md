Field Actions provide the player with abilities they will need to navigate the world. Each Combat Character recruited provides a new Field Action, and the character does not need to be in the current Party for their Field Action to be available.

## Solving Puzzles
Field Actions are often used to solve puzzles in the world, both on the critical path and in secret areas.

## Revisiting Areas
The player will often see things on the Field Map that they cannot interact with at the current time and will need to revisit once they acquire the necessary Field Action.

## Field Actions List

| Field Action | Effect                                                                                                                         | Provided By | Example Use                                                         |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Slice        | Slices the air in front of the player. Can cut ropes, vines, etc.                                                              | Soldier     | Cut a rope to drop a platform.                                      |
| Strength     | Lifts the object in front of the player (specific objects only). Player can then drop the object in a cardinal direction.      | Barbarian   | Lift and move a boulder blocking the path.                          |
| Cleanse      | Removes infection in front of the player. Can cure disease, remove alien contagion, etc.                                       | Cleric      | Cure an NPC. Remove contagion from a ladder.                        |
| Rift Jump    | Activates a Rift in front of the player, performing a short range teleport to a new area.                                      | Occultist   | Use Rifts to navigate broken path.                                  |
| Recall       | Provides the "Recall" option in the Menu, teleporting the player back to the last Campfire they used.                          | Witch       | Utility only.                                                       |
| Ignite       | Cast a flame in front of the player. Can light torches, spark fuses, melt ice, etc.                                            | Mage        | Light the fuse to a cannon to destroy an obstacle.                  |
| Dig          | Dig into the ground in front of the player. If the ground can be dug, a hole will be shown and treasure may be generated.      | Druid       | Dig up a lost ring for an NPC so they open the path.                |
| Lockpick     | Pick a lock in front of the player. Doors, chests, traps, etc. can be picked.                                                  | Thief       | Pick a lock blocking the path. Jam a trap blocking the path.        |
| Mercantile   | Provides the "Special Stock" option when at some shops. Special Stock items are often limited.                                 | Bard        | Player needs access to Special Stock to buy a key to open the path. |
| Efficiency   | When using a Consumable item, there is a 30% chance the item will not be consumed.                                             | Alchemist   | Utility only.                                                       |
| Quick Shot   | Fires a slingshot in front of the player. The slingshot can hit buttons and stun enemy NPCs (does not guarantee first strike). | Archer      | Shoot a button through a grate to open a door.                      |
| Break        | Perform a shattering punch in front of the player. Cracked walls can be broken.                                                | Monk        | Break a cracked wall hiding a switch to open a door.                |
|              |                                                                                                                                |             |                                                                     |
