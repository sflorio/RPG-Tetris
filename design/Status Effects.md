Status Effects are temporary boons or ailments that are applied in combat. Status Effects can be applied to individual units, allies, enemies, and the boards.

Similar to Stat Modifiers, Status Effects can also be enhanced, or weakened, through the addition of + and - icons.
## Unit Effects
Unit Effects can only be applied to units, but can be applied equally to allies and enemies. Some Unit Effects will affect the Board as well, with the effect being dependent on the unit's position within the team.

## Compound Effects
In some situations, multiple effects being applied to the same target can cause unexpected results.

| Effect A | Effect B | Outcome                   |
| -------- | -------- | ------------------------- |
| Renew    | Bleed    | Both effects are cleared. |
| Renew    | Poison   | Both effects are cleared. |
