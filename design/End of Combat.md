After combat is completed, the player is rewarded with progression and has a chance to receive currency and/or items.

All status effects are cleared at the end of combat, but characters do not return to maximum HP and spent Tech Points are not regained.
### Experience and Leveling Up
Each enemy defeated is worth a certain amount of Experience Points and Mastery Points; Experience Points increase the Party Level, and Mastery Points are used by characters to improve their abilities. All characters the player has recruited are always the same level, and the Party always receives full Experience Points. 
### Mastery Points
Mastery Points are used by characters to level up their Basic Attack, Advanced Attack, Techniques and Rally Strike.

Only characters that participated in the Active Team for at least one round will receive Mastery Points.
### Loot
Defeated enemies can also drop loot, which falls into one of the following categories:
- Unidentified Soul Gems (??? Soul Gems)
- Crowns (currency)
- Xenoshards (trade item/collectible)
- Consumables
	- If there is not room in the Backpack, the Consumables dropped can be used immediately, discarded or swapped for another Consumable in the Backpack. The swapped item is discarded
- Keepsakes
	- Some quests may require the player to defeat enemies and acquire items through loot
